> [!note] Definition
> Formally, if $f(x)$ is continuous on $[a, b]$, and  
> $$
> \int_{a}^{b} f(x) \eta(x) \, dx = 0
> $$
> for all smooth test functions $η(x)$ where $η(a) = η(b) = 0$, then:
> $$
> f(x) = 0 \quad \text{for all } x \in [a, b]
> $$
