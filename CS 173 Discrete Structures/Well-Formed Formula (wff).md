> [!definition]
> ## Well-Formed Formula
> We say $\lambda$ is a wff iff (by definintion) $\lambda$ syntactically looks like any of the following expressions
> 1. $\phi(t_0, t_1, ..., t_{n-1})$
>    Where $\phi$ is an n-ary predicate, n is a natural number, and $t_0, t_1, ..., t_{n-1}$ are terms
> 2. $\top$
> 3. $\bot$
> 4. $\neg (\phi)$ where $\phi$ is a wff
> 5. $\phi \land \psi$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
> 6. $\phi \lor \psi$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
> 7. $\phi \implies \psi$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
> 8. $\phi \iff \psi$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
> 9. $\forall (\phi)$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
> 10. $\exists (\phi)$ where $\phi, \psi$ are wff
